Verify a BPST Academy certificate

Employers, colleges and parents can check any certificate or letter issued by BPST Software Education (BPST Academy), Lucknow. Enter the number printed on it, or scan its QR code.

Format: BPST-C-YYYY-NNNN (courses) or BPST-E-YYYY-NNNN (experience letters). Try the sample: BPST-C-2026-0187.
That doesn't look like a BPST certificate number.

Questions

How do I check if a BPST Academy certificate is real?

Enter the certificate number printed on the certificate (for example BPST-C-2026-0187) above, or scan the QR code on it. A genuine certificate shows the learner's name, course, dates and grade.

Which documents can be verified here?

Course completion certificates, industrial and summer/winter training certificates, internship or project completion letters, and staff experience letters issued by BPST Academy, Lucknow.

What if the certificate is not found?

Check the number for typing mistakes. If it is still not found, call or WhatsApp +91 82991 01616 or email bitprosofttech@gmail.com before accepting the document.
