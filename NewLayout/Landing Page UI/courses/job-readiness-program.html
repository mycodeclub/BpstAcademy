What Is the Job-Readiness Program Course?

Skills get you in, preparation keeps you ahead. Personality development, communication, mock interviews, ATS-friendly resumes, Naukri and LinkedIn profiles and a job-hunting strategy — the six modules interviewers notice first.

Who is it for?

Final-year students, freshers and graduates preparing for campus or off-campus placements.

Prerequisites

No prior experience needed — we start from fundamentals.

Job-Readiness Program Syllabus — Module by Module

Module 01
Personality Development (PDP)
  • Self-introduction & elevator pitch
  • Body language & grooming
  • Time management & goal setting
  • Workplace etiquette & email writing
  • Team work & leadership activities
  • Stress & interview-anxiety handling
Module 02
Communication Skills
  • Spoken English practice sessions
  • Technical articulation — explain your project
  • Group discussion (GD) practice
  • Presentations & demo day
  • Listening & answering structure (STAR)
  • Professional writing: emails, reports
Module 03
Mock Interviews
  • Technical interview on your stack
  • Coding round under time limit
  • Project deep-dive questions
  • System design basics (for 6-month)
  • HR round — salary, relocation, strengths
  • Written feedback & improvement plan
Module 04
Resume Building
  • ATS-friendly format & keywords
  • Project descriptions with impact
  • GitHub & live project links
  • Tailored versions per role
  • Cover letter template
  • Final review by mentor
Module 05
Naukri & LinkedIn Profiles
  • Naukri profile creation & resume headline
  • Key-skills & profile-summary optimisation
  • Regular Naukri profile refresh (stay on top)
  • LinkedIn headline, about & featured projects
  • Networking & recruiter outreach templates
  • GitHub profile README & pinned repos
Module 06
Job Hunting & Placement Support
  • Job-search strategy & daily application plan
  • Portals: Naukri, LinkedIn, Indeed, Instahyre
  • Referrals & hiring-drive connections
  • Interview scheduling & preparation per company
  • Offer comparison & negotiation guidance
  • Follow-up after course completion
